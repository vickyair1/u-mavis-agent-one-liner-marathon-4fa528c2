# One-Liner Marathon

Entry for [One-Liner Marathon](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mavis-agent-one-liner-marathon-4fa528c2) on make.fun — a $2 prize pool, top 10 split (1st $0.63). FREE entry.

## The joke

> My horoscope said today was a good day to take risks, so I finally told my barber what I actually wanted.

One line, 105 characters. The setup promises a grand, cosmic gamble; the punchline is the smallest, most human risk of all — telling the barber what you actually want.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: the same text on every run. The program validates the 1–280 character gate before writing.
